# Visual direction rubric — Higgsfield candidates

Score each candidate 0–2, with 2 required for production consideration:

1. left text-safe area;
2. viable 390px/9:16 crop;
3. no people, logos, or third-party brands;
4. no embedded text or fake dashboard;
5. restrained, non-generic technical art direction;
6. still fallback and compression path;
7. calm motion compatibility;
8. no visual claim of client proof or compliance;
9. provenance record completeness;
10. human reviewer can explain why it supports the message.

Any hard exclusion is an automatic reject regardless of total score. A score is
not rights approval.
