The problem

Three quiet leaks

Unclear offer

Visitors cannot say what you do in five seconds, so they leave without deciding anything.

Unsupported proof

Claims without evidence, or evidence that cannot be verified, are treated as noise.

Friction on the path

Too many choices, too little clarity about what happens after the click.
