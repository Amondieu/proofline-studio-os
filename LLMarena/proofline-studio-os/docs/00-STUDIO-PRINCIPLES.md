# 00 — Studio Principles
<!-- v0.1 · 2026-09-23 · owner: founder (sign-off required) · change process: ADR in docs/adr/ -->

1. **We sell clarity, not decoration.** If a design change cannot be explained as clarity, trust or path-to-action, it needs a written reason or it does not ship.
2. **Every claim has an evidence type.** One of: *demonstrated method · stated standard · permissioned quote · labelled concept work*. A claim without a type is deleted, not softened.
3. **We never manufacture proof.** No invented testimonials, logos, metrics, awards, team photos or synthetic staff/customers.
4. **We research patterns, never expression.** No brand's layout, copy, illustration style, logo or screenshots enter a deliverable. Concept work is labelled: "Studio Concept — independent demonstration, not client work."
5. **Licence safety is a design constraint, not paperwork.** If the licence is unclear → DO NOT SHIP UNTIL CONFIRMED.
6. **Accessibility is part of done.** WCAG 2.2 AA is the target, measured by automated checks *and* a manual keyboard sweep. We say "we build to WCAG 2.2 AA and document the evidence", never "we are compliant".
7. **Performance is a promise with numbers.** LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1 at p75. Motion is a budget, not a default.
8. **The client owns everything.** Repo, hosting, domain, analytics, CMS, content. We hold delegated access, never ownership.
9. **Fixed scope, visible trade-offs.** Revision limits, content freeze dates and change-request pricing are stated before the project starts.
10. **Humans gate the irreversible.** No publishing, DNS change, email send, dependency install into production, client-data upload, terms acceptance, spend, or compliance claim happens without an explicit founder approval.
11. **We keep a receipt.** Launch receipts, QA receipts, approval records and provenance logs are part of the deliverable, not internal paperwork.
12. **We improve the system, not the tool list.** Every project produces at most one new reusable artefact and at least one deleted assumption.
