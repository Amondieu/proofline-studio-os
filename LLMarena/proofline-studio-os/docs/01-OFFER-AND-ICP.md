## Offer ladder
| Tier | Price | Scope (hard limits) | Timeline | Not included |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter Conversion Page | €500–750 | 1 page, ≤7 sections, copy edit (not research), 1 direction, 1 revision round, form, analytics, QA receipt | 5 working days | Brand identity, illustration, CMS, copywriting from scratch |
| Authority Landing Sprint | €1,250–1,750 | 1–2 pages, message map, 1 direction + 1 alternate token pass, motion budget, 2 revision rounds, booking flow, editor training (30 min) | 10 working days | Multi-language, custom photography, A/B testing |
| Launch System | €2,500–3,000 | ≤5 pages + lightweight CMS, full message architecture, teardown-quality audit of the old site, 3 revision rounds, analytics + event taxonomy, 60-day check-in | 14 working days | Copy for >5 pages, integrations beyond form/booking/analytics |

## ICP 1 — B2B AI & automation consultants
- **Trigger:** they can explain their offer in a call but the site converts poorly; they have evidence (case notes, demos) but no narrative.
- **Pain:** credibility gap ("looks like a side project"), unclear offer, no proof structure.
- **Disqualifiers:** no existing clients/references at all, wants "viral" landing pages, cannot name a decision maker.
- **Best direction:** Precision System.

## ICP 2 — Micro-SaaS / early-stage SaaS founders
- **Trigger:** pre-launch or post-launch with traffic but weak activation; founder writes all copy at 1 a.m.
- **Pain:** messaging sprawl, feature-led pages, no path from visit to trial.
- **Disqualifiers:** needs app/dashboard work (refer out), needs auth/billing, expects "growth" guarantees.
- **Best direction:** Precision System (or Cinematic for launch moments).

## ICP 3 — Premium creative & digital service providers
- **Trigger:** repositioning upward; current site looks cheaper than their prices.
- **Pain:** visual language contradicts the price point; no editorial restraint.
- **Disqualifiers:** wants a brand identity from scratch (needs a brand studio), wants stock-photo-heavy design.
- **Best direction:** Editorial Luxury (Cinematic for launch campaigns).

## Proof position (be honest)
We have no permissioned client case studies yet. Therefore:
- Portfolio shows **labelled concept work** and **method receipts** (gates, budgets, provenance, ownership model).
- Client work appears only after written permission, verbatim quotes, and a completed case-study template.
- We do not use "trusted by", client logo walls, or invented numbers — ever.

## Capacity model
Max 2 concurrent projects, max 3/month at Starter tier, max 6 delivery days committed per week (1 day reserved for sales + system).
