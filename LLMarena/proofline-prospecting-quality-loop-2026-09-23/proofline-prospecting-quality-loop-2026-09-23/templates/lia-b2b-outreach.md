# Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) — B2B Outreach

## 1. Purpose test — why we want to process the data
- Interest: growing a one-person studio by offering a directly relevant professional service.
- Benefit: the recipient gets a specific, free, factual observation about their own public page.
- Is the interest lawful and clearly articulated? ☐ yes
- Would a reasonable person in the recipient's role see this as expected professional contact? ☐ yes / ☐ no → if no, **do not send**

## 2. Necessity test — is this the least intrusive way?
| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Inbound content/SEO only | slow, no direct feedback loop |
| Recruit via communities/referrals | no control over timing; used in parallel, not replaced |
| Public social post tagging the company | less private than a direct — actually more intrusive publicly |
| Buying a contact database | disproportionate: we cannot satisfy Art. 14 information honestly |
Conclusion: a single, function-related, personalised email to a **publicly listed business address** is the least intrusive effective option. Amount of data: minimal (see `docs/30 §D`).

## 3. Balancing test — the recipient's rights
| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Nature of data | business contact data only (company, role, public business email, public URL) |
| Reasonable expectations | business emails are routinely used for genuinely relevant professional contact |
| Impact of the processing | low: one-off, ≤3 touches, no profiling, no sensitive inference, no automated decisions |
| Vulnerable individuals | none targeted; exclude sole traders using personal addresses and any regulated/public-office context without review |
| Consequences of objection | **immediate and permanent** suppression of the person + domain; no re-import from any other file |
| Transparency | source, purpose, rights and a one-click objection in every message (Art. 14) |
| Retention | firmographics 12 months; signals 90 days; inactive prospects deleted after 3 years; suppression kept indefinitely as necessary for compliance |
**Outcome:** interests do not override the rights and freedoms of the recipient, given the safeguards above. ☐ agreed

## 4. Safeguards in place (all must be checked)
☐ Send Gate enforced per message · ☐ suppression list is a separate system of record · ☐ ≤3 touches then stop · ☐ no sensitive data or inferences · ☐ no automated decision-making · ☐ opt-out honoured within 24 h · ☐ annual re-review of this LIA and of every data source.
