# Quick 3-Point Teardown (5–12 minutes, outbound version)
<!-- v0.1 · per prospect · public pages only · no client material · optional: paywalled-access pages are never examined -->
## 0. Preconditions
- [ ] Page is publicly accessible without login.
- [ ] I am looking at **mobile first** (real device or 375 px viewport), then desktop.
- [ ] I have ≤12 minutes. If I cannot find three honest findings, I do **not** send this prospect anything.
- [ ] No claim in my teardown is unverifiable, and none of it insults the work.

## 1. The three checks (in this order)

**A · Hero clarity** — can a stranger answer, within five seconds: what is this, who is it for, what happens if I click?
> Currently clear: ____________________ · Missing: ____________________
> Evidence: exact text quoted (≤25 words) + URL

**B · Proof / risk** — is there a credible decision aid (method, standard, real permissioned quote, labelled concept) near the point of decision?
> Currently present: ____________________ · Missing or misplaced: ____________________
> Evidence: URL + what is actually on the page (no speculation)

**C · Action path** — on mobile, can a motivated visitor act in one obvious step, and is the form/booking flow free of unnecessary fields?
> Friction observed (field count, unclear next step, scroll distance): ____________________
> Evidence: reproduced by me at 375 px on <date>

## 2. Deliverable (choose one form)
- **90-second Loom** — screen recording, mobile viewport, the three findings in order, no jargon.
- **150–250 words text** — same structure, plain text, no attachments.

**Structure (both forms):** finding → what a visitor experiences → the smallest fix → one next step.
Then the **hero rewrite**: one alternative `<h1>` + one supporting line + one CTA label.

## 3. Metrics to log (per prospect, in the Notion record)
`audit_completed_at` · `delivery_form` (loom|text) · `reply_outcome` (positive|neutral|negative|none|optout) · `call_booked` (y/n) · `minutes_spent`

## 4. What this teardown is NOT
Not a free redesign · not an accessibility audit (that requires the full manual sweep and the client's scope) · not a performance audit (that requires field data) · not a promise of results · not a licence to criticise the client's team or their previous agency.

## 5. Reuse rule
If a finding repeats across ≥5 prospects in one ICP slice, it becomes a **candidate pattern card** in `docs/31` and possibly a published "what we keep seeing" article. That is how outreach feeds the learning loop instead of just consuming time.
