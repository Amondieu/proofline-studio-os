# Austria business-readiness note

<!-- status: research / not legal advice / jurisdiction: Austria / reviewed: 2026-09-23 -->

This is an internal operating note for Proofline Studio. It records questions
and gates for an Austria-based website and landing-page studio. It is not a
legal opinion, a Gewerbeanmeldung, tax advice, privacy notice, or substitute
for a review by the WKO Gründerservice, the competent authority, a tax adviser,
or a lawyer.

## Working scope

The planned activity may combine:

- conception and visual design of websites and landing pages;
- conversion-oriented copy and information architecture;
- technical implementation and deployment support;
- limited integrations or routing, if separately scoped.

The exact Gewerbewortlaut and any boundary between design, advertising,
copy/conception, IT services, and other advisory work remain open. Do not infer
the final classification from a template or from this note.

## Facts to verify before paid work

The official Austrian guidance describes a self-employed activity as
gewerbsmäßig when it is carried out independently, regularly or with an intent
to repeat, and with an intent to obtain economic advantage. A Gewerbe may only
be exercised with the required Gewerbeberechtigung. See
[oesterreich.gv.at — Wann wird eine Tätigkeit als gewerbsmäßig eingestuft?](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/arbeit_beruf_und_pension/selbststaendigkeit/13/Seite.1090200)
and the [oesterreich.gv.at Gewerbe definition](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/lexicon/G/Seite.991598).

For internet activity, the WKO notes that activities remain subject to the
Gewerbeordnung when they are carried out commercially and that a corresponding
Gewerbeberechtigung may already be relevant when the activity is first offered
online. Its guidance also distinguishes visual website design from technical
implementation. See [WKO — Gewerberecht und Internet](https://www.wko.at/internetrecht/gewerberecht-und-internet).

The WKO describes `Werbegrafik-Designer` as a free-trade wording covering,
among other things, digital media and web/app design, while its IT guidance
describes `Dienstleistungen in der automatischen Datenverarbeitung und
Informationstechnik`. These are investigation leads, not a classification
decision for this studio. Confirm the exact wording and combined scope with
the regional WKO and competent authority. See [WKO — Kommunikationsdesign](https://www.wko.at/information-consulting/werbung-marktkommunikation/kommunikationsdesign)
and [WKO — Unternehmensgründung für Informationstechnologen](https://www.wko.at/information-consulting/unternehmensberatung-buchhaltung-informationstechnologie/it-dienstleistung/unternehmensgruendung).

### Free Gewerbe is not a registration exemption

The WKO describes free Gewerbe as activities for which no special
qualification proof such as an examination is generally required. That does
not remove the need to register the activity, meet the general personal
requirements, or stay within the exact registered scope. See
[WKO — Befähigungsnachweis](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/befaehigungsnachweis)
and [WKO — Voraussetzungen für die Gewerbeberechtigung](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/voraussetzungen-erlangung-gewerbeberechtigung).

For Proofline, the following are only classification questions for the WKO,
not conclusions:

| Planned activity | Investigation lead | Must be confirmed |
|---|---|---|
| Website/Landing-Page programming, deployment, integrations, maintenance | IT services | Whether the concrete technical scope, forms, integrations, and maintenance are covered |
| UI/Webdesign and visual layout | Werbegrafik-Designer / communication design | Which wording covers the actual design and production scope |
| Conversion copy, offer structure, and landing-page optimization | advertising/copy/conception or advisory boundary | Whether it is ancillary to the registered service or requires a separate wording |
| Hosting or account operation | IT service / separate operational responsibility | Who owns accounts, credentials, data processing, and liability |

Do not market the studio as “officially qualified”, “certified”, or covered by
a particular Gewerbe until the wording has been confirmed for the actual
service mix.

### Working two-track option for WKO clarification

Based on the current WKO profiles, the most useful clarification request for
the combined WITHKODEX/Proofline scope is whether these two free-trade wordings
are needed, sufficient, or need narrowing:

1. `Werbeagentur, Multimediaagentur` for online-agency work such as webdesign,
   information architecture, UX/UI optimisation, content, and the technical
   implementation of digital communication surfaces; and
2. `Dienstleistungen in der automatischen Datenverarbeitung und
   Informationstechnik` for software, integrations, data processing, AI/system
   implementation, and technical maintenance.

The [WKO online-agency profile](https://www.wko.at/information-consulting/werbung-marktkommunikation/online-agentur)
lists both creative and technical online-agency activities. The [WKO IT
profile](https://www.wko.at/vlbg/information-consulting/unternehmensberatung-buchhaltung-informationstechnologie/infomappe-it-3.pdf)
describes the IT service scope. These profiles are evidence for questions to
ask, not permission to self-classify or an instruction to register both.

`Werbegrafik-Designer` remains a separate design-related clarification lead if
graphic/brand design becomes an independent core service. Dataset products and
licensing require their own product, source, rights, tax, and contract review.

Do not use `Unternehmensberatung einschließlich Unternehmensorganisation` as a
casual umbrella label for strategy, management, or organisation work. The WKO
identifies that field with a qualification/examination path; keep Proofline
and WITHKODEX copy anchored in concrete design, communication, IT, integration,
and implementation work until a separate qualification review says otherwise.
See [WKO — Unternehmensberatung](https://www.wko.at/noe/bildung-lehre/ausscheibung-bp-unternehmensberatung).

### WKO clarification request

Use this as a starting draft for the regional WKO Gründerservice. It is a
questionnaire, not a legal filing and must be adapted to the actual facts:

```text
Betreff: Gewerbewortlaut für Webdesign- und Landing-Page-Studio

Ich wohne in Österreich und möchte als Einzelunternehmer unter der
Geschäftsbezeichnung „WITHKODEX“ B2B-Websites, Landing Pages und technische
AI-/IT-Leistungen anbieten. Mein tatsächlicher Leistungsumfang umfasst:

- Konzeption und Informationsarchitektur
- UI-/Webdesign und visuelle Gestaltung
- technische Umsetzung, Deployment und laufende Wartung
- Formulare sowie Kalender-, CRM- und Analytics-Integrationen
- API-/Schnittstellenintegration und technische Automatisierung
- AI-/Agent-/MCP-Prototyping, Datenverarbeitung und technische Dokumentation
- conversion-orientierte Copy und Optimierung digitaler Kommunikationsflächen

Bitte teilen Sie mir mit, ob die freien Gewerbe bzw. Gewerbewortlaute
„Werbeagentur, Multimediaagentur“ und
„Dienstleistungen in der automatischen Datenverarbeitung und
Informationstechnik“ diesen konkreten Leistungsumfang abdecken.

Bitte weisen Sie darauf hin, ob für UI-/Webdesign, Copy/Conversion-Arbeit,
Dataset-/Lizenzprodukte oder einzelne Integrationen eine zusätzliche
Abgrenzung oder Anmeldung erforderlich ist.

Ich biete keine Rechts-, Steuer-, Finanz-, medizinische oder allgemeine
Unternehmensberatung und keine verbindliche Compliance-Zertifizierung an.
```

Record the written response, date, contact, exact scope discussed, and any
limitations as evidence for `G-BIZ-001`. A general web page or AI answer is
not a substitute for that scope-specific confirmation.

For a website, Austrian information and disclosure duties depend on the
operator, legal form, content, and medium. The official portal points to
website information duties and the Mediengesetz/ECG context; it also warns
that external images, music, video, and identifiable people require rights
attention. See [USP — Rechtliche Pflichten für Websites](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/betrieb-und-umwelt/laufender-betrieb/weitere-informationen-laufender-betrieb/e-commerce-kompetenz/anleitungen-um-das-geschaeft-online-zu-bringen/rechtliche-pflichten-fuer-websites)
and [USP — Impressumspflicht gemäß § 24 Mediengesetz](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/brancheninformationen/information-und-kommunikation/impressumspflicht-gemaess-para-24-mediengesetz.html).

Invoice obligations and required invoice fields depend on the transaction,
customer, amount, tax treatment, and cross-border facts. Use the official
[USP invoice guidance](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/rechnung.html)
as a starting point and obtain tax confirmation before issuing an invoice.

For contact forms and non-essential analytics/cookies, the actual data flows,
purposes, recipients, retention, providers, and consent mechanism must be
documented before activation. See [WKO — Datenschutzerklärung: Checkliste und Informationspflichten](https://www.wko.at/internetrecht/datenschutzerklaerung-checkliste-infopflichten-dsgvo-tkg-we).

## Two-phase operating boundary

### Phase A — research / concept only

Allowed only when the presentation is genuinely non-commercial and does not
pretend that a paid service is available:

- build and privately review the studio concept and portfolio demonstrators;
- conduct market interviews and fit conversations;
- prepare draft scopes, source research, QA tooling, and internal templates;
- create a clearly free research/audit flow only after privacy handling is
  reviewed;
- collect no payment, deposit, invoice, or binding paid acceptance.

Because official WKO guidance treats public online advertising as relevant to
the commercial activity question, do not assume that a public service page,
price list, booking flow, or paid call-to-action is automatically covered by a
pre-registration “test phase”. Get local confirmation before publishing such
an offer.

### Phase B — registered paid delivery

Before any binding paid offer acceptance, contract, invoice, deposit, paid
delivery, or public paid checkout, the following gate must be human-approved:

1. exact activity and Gewerbewortlaut confirmed with the responsible Austrian
   contact;
2. registration status and business identity recorded;
3. invoice/tax/SVS/WKO action list reviewed for the actual situation;
4. reviewed legal and privacy pages prepared for the real operator and data
   flows;
5. SOW/contract reviewed for scope, payment, rights, confidentiality,
   responsibility, and handover;
6. asset/claim/AI rights register active;
7. Proofline human gates and ownership handover active.

## Out of scope for this note

- selecting a final Gewerbe without human confirmation;
- drafting final Impressum, privacy notice, cookie text, or contract terms;
- deciding tax residency, VAT, SVS, WKO, or cross-border treatment;
- determining whether a particular prospecting message is lawful;
- approving a public paid launch.
