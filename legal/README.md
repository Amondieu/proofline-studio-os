# Legal and business readiness

This directory contains jurisdiction-specific research notes, decision records,
and pre-launch gates. It does not contain legal advice or final legal text.

## Current operating assumption

The working jurisdiction for Proofline Studio is Austria. The operative note is
[`AUSTRIA-BUSINESS-READINESS.md`](AUSTRIA-BUSINESS-READINESS.md).

The earlier France-oriented material is not an Austrian operating baseline. It
must be re-evaluated if residence, establishment, client location, or delivery
model changes.

## Document states

`research` → `draft` → `professionally reviewed` → `approved for the named
jurisdiction` → `active`

No document in this directory is `active` by default. Placeholders for legal
identity, registration, tax treatment, privacy, or contractual language must
not be presented as complete legal pages.

## Authority boundary

- The repository may preserve questions, source links, checklists, and gates.
- It must not determine the correct Austrian Gewerbewortlaut, tax treatment,
  privacy legal basis, or final contract language autonomously.
- A qualified Austrian review is required before paid public offers, contracts,
  invoices, data-collecting forms, or production delivery.
- The business-registration gate can block work; it cannot approve itself.
