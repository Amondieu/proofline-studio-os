## Decision

- Buyer and situation: `[DISCOVERY: identify the person, stakes, trigger, and fit boundary]`
- Point of view: `[MESSAGE: one precise belief that changes the buyer's next decision]`
- Primary CTA: Apply for a fit call
- Secondary CTA: Read a point-of-view note
- Engagement boundary: `[DISCOVERY: scope, exclusions, delivery model, and first useful outcome]`

## Page sequence

1. **Point-of-view hero** — lead with the argument, not a credential dump. `[PROOF: source, experience, or clearly labelled perspective]`
2. **Who it is for** — describe fit, non-fit, and the moment when help is useful.
3. **Method** — make the working model and client participation concrete. `[PROOF: documented method]`
4. **Selected proof** — include role, scope, context, and permission for every case or testimonial.
5. **Engagement model** — show stages, outputs, time, responsibilities, and boundary conditions.
6. **About** — support trust with relevant experience; do not let biography carry the whole decision.
7. **Fit questions and application CTA** — collect only necessary context and state what happens next.

## Build and QA contract

- Avoid vague transformation language, manufactured scarcity, unconsented testimonials, and personal-brand clichés.
- Use quiet transitions and strong reading order; test keyboard, focus, reduced motion, contrast, and 390px layout.
- Performance budget: `[PLAN: budgets and measured result]`; evidence source: `[EVIDENCE: lab/RUM result]`.
- Accessibility: `[QA: WCAG criteria, manual checks, and unresolved findings]`.
- Application form: `[BUILD: required fields, consent, privacy purpose, response owner, error/retry state]`.
- Claims, credentials, awards, client names, and outcomes need a source, permission, and review date.
