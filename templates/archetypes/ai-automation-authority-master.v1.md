## Page sequence

1. **Outcome-first hero** — name the workflow and next decision. `[PROOF: approved capability or constraint]`
2. **Workflow diagnosis** — show where friction, risk, or repetition occurs. `[EVIDENCE: client-approved process evidence or clearly labelled hypothesis]`
3. **Mechanism** — explain inputs, orchestration, human control, and handoffs in buyer language. `[PROOF: documented method; no fake system diagram]`
4. **Proof boundary** — separate shipped work, prototype, and concept. `[RIGHTS/CLAIMS: verify permission, source, date, and scope]`
5. **Engagement path** — show assessment inputs, time, owner, output, and what happens next.
6. **Objections and constraints** — integrations, data handling, implementation risk, maintenance, and non-fit.
7. **Assessment CTA** — form asks only for information needed to qualify the workflow. Include consent and recovery state.
