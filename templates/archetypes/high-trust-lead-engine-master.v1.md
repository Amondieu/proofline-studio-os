## Page sequence

1. **Trust-first hero** — name the situation and safe next step without fear or urgency.
2. **Situation fit** — explain who should contact the provider and who should use another route.
3. **What happens next** — show owner, timing, contact method, and what will not happen.
4. **Proof and credentials** — use verified credentials, process evidence, and permissioned client proof.
5. **Privacy boundary** — place consent, data purpose, retention, and sensitive-data warnings before collection.
6. **FAQ and legal layer** — answer pressure, scope, cost, and escalation questions using approved legal text.
7. **Contact route** — offer a low-risk question path and a consultation path with clear success/error/retry states.

## Build and QA contract

- No fear-based urgency, dark-pattern consent, unverified badges, premature sensitive data, or regulated guarantees.
- Minimal motion; prioritize calm reading order, focus, consent clarity, contrast, keyboard access, and 390px layout.
- Performance budget: `[PLAN: budgets and measured result]`; evidence source: `[EVIDENCE: lab/RUM result]`.
- Accessibility: `[QA: WCAG criteria, manual checks, and unresolved findings]`.
- Legal/privacy: `[LEGAL OWNER: approved text, jurisdiction, version, and review date]`; agents may not invent it.
- Form: `[BUILD: purpose-limited fields, consent record, routing owner, abuse protection, recovery path]`.
