## Page sequence

1. **Use-case hero** — make the product category and decision outcome legible. `[PROOF: real product capability]`
2. **Problem cost** — show the current workflow and consequence without fabricated numbers. `[EVIDENCE: approved research or labelled hypothesis]`
3. **Product mechanism** — connect feature to job, workflow, and result. `[PROOF: real interface or labelled prototype]`
4. **Guided walkthrough** — reveal the smallest credible path from input to outcome; keep an accessible static alternative.
5. **Fit and comparison** — say who it is for, who it is not for, and what adoption requires.
6. **Proof and trust** — customer evidence, security facts, integrations, and product status only when verified and permissioned.
7. **Demo/trial CTA** — explain qualification, response expectation, and next step; include success/error/retry states.

## Build and QA contract

- No feature wall above the fold, fake product UI, unbounded claims, or motion before message.
- Protect first meaningful paint, reduced motion, keyboard access, focus visibility, and 390px readability.
- Performance budget: `[PLAN: budgets and measured result]`; evidence source: `[EVIDENCE: lab/RUM result]`.
- Accessibility: `[QA: WCAG criteria, manual checks, and unresolved findings]`.
- Trial/demo path: `[BUILD: real route, qualification fields, privacy purpose, recovery path]`.
- Security, pricing, integrations, customer logos, and metrics require an evidence owner and review date.
