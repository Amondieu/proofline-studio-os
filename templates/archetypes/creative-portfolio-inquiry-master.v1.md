## Page sequence

1. **Point-of-view hero** — establish the studio's lens and inquiry promise.
2. **Selected work** — curate a small set of relevant projects; every item has rights and a clear role boundary.
3. **Case context** — show brief, contribution, constraints, and outcome only where approved. `[PROOF/RIGHTS: source and permission]`
4. **Capabilities** — translate craft into buyer-relevant deliverables and exclusions.
5. **Process** — show collaboration, review points, inputs, and handoff.
6. **Fit and constraints** — explain project types, timing, capacity, and non-fit.
7. **Inquiry CTA** — collect a concise brief, privacy purpose, response expectation, and recovery path.

## Build and QA contract

- No unlicensed work, masonry overload, hover-only meaning, autoplay sound, or case study without role/context.
- Motion may clarify work but must have a readable still path; protect mobile performance and reduced motion.
- Performance budget: `[PLAN: budgets and measured result]`; evidence source: `[EVIDENCE: lab/RUM result]`.
- Accessibility: `[QA: WCAG criteria, alt text, focus order, keyboard path, and unresolved findings]`.
- Inquiry: `[BUILD: brief fields, attachment policy, consent, validation, success/error/retry states]`.
- Credits, collaborators, images, fonts, music, and client work have rights owners and usage decisions.
