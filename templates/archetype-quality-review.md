master-template candidate. It records evidence and limitations; it does not

## Hard gates

- [ ] Normative accessibility requirements checked against the current standard.
- [ ] Core performance path has a measurable budget and a field/lab distinction.
- [ ] Form, privacy, rights, and claim boundaries are known or explicitly open.
- [ ] Primary action works and the page has a recoverable failure path.
- [ ] No award, gallery, case-study metric, or community opinion is treated as a gate by itself.
