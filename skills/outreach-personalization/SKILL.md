# Outreach personalization envelope

Read `docs/strategy/27-COLD-EMAIL-PLAYBOOK.md` and
`docs/strategy/26-OUTREACH-COMPLIANCE-FR-EU.md` first.

Use the installed upstream `cold-email`, `copywriting`, and `product-marketing`
references only for draft quality. Personalisation must be traceable to public
evidence and must not invent familiarity, metrics, pain, or proof. Include a
transparent sender identity and reviewed objection route.

Output an `OutreachDraft` with `status=draft_only` and
`human_review_required=true`. Never send or schedule a message.
