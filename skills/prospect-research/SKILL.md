# Prospect research envelope

Read `docs/strategy/23-OUTBOUND-INTELLIGENCE-SYSTEM.md`, `24-ICP-AND-SIGNAL-MODELS.md`,
`25-PROSPECT-SCORING.md`, and `config/studio/outbound-policy.v1.json` first.

Use the installed upstream `prospecting`, `customer-research`,
`competitor-profiling`, and `product-marketing` references only as drafting
helpers. Store source URL, source type, collection date, terms review, and
confidence. Never guess contact data, bypass controls, collect sensitive data,
or export a live contact list.

Output `ProspectRecord` candidates and stop at human review. Do not draft or
send outreach automatically.
