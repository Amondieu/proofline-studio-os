# Landing Studio execution envelope

Use this skill for any work that creates or changes a client website, landing
page, studio showcase, conversion copy, creative direction, or launch review.

## Always retrieve

1. `docs/architecture/STUDIO-OPERATING-MODEL.md`
2. `docs/architecture/LANDING-STUDIO-HARNESS.md`
3. the relevant recipe in `docs/strategy/`
4. the relevant template in `docs/operations/`

## Required behavior

- Establish the primary audience and one conversion before designing.
- Separate supplied proof, assumptions, and concepts.
- Freeze the message map before producing visual systems.
- Use one controlled creative direction and review its mobile crop.
- Test all required widths, keyboard path, reduced motion, forms, metadata,
  rights, privacy links, consent, and backup/export.
- Keep the client in control of domains, accounts, analytics, and publishing.
- Use the harness as a blocking explanation layer, never as a launch authority.

## Prohibited behavior

- Do not invent testimonials, logos, numbers, customer stories, or legal claims.
- Do not call a prototype a production-ready build.
- Do not upload confidential client information or credentials to creative tools.
- Do not add live provider credentials, scheduler authority, or deployment
  commands to this foundation slice.
