## Core message

- **Audience:** technical AI and automation offers that need a clearer path from
  capability to qualified conversation.
- **Job-to-be-done:** make a complex offer legible, credible, and easy to act on
  without flattening its technical substance.
- **Core pain:** the offer is understood by its creator but not quickly enough
  by a new visitor.
- **Mechanism:** discovery, message architecture, one governed creative
  direction, implementation, and evidence-led QA.
- **Promise:** a clearer decision path and a more credible presentation of the
  offer; no unsupported promise about leads or revenue.
- **Primary CTA:** `Request an AI / Automation teardown`.
- **CTA qualification:** the form is a local demo until a reviewed endpoint,
  retention policy, and notification path exist.
