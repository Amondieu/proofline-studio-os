# Discovery brief — first archetype portfolio

## Gate status

`DRAFT — human confirmation required`

This is the first studio-owned archetype surface. It demonstrates how
Proofline would frame an AI or automation offer; it does not represent a
commissioned client engagement.

## Working brief

- **Audience:** technical founders, productized AI/automation specialists, and
  small consultancies whose offer is valuable but difficult to explain quickly.
- **Buyer job:** understand whether the studio can turn a complex offer into a
  credible, conversion-oriented website direction.
- **Problem:** technical capability is often clearer to the builder than to a
  prospective buyer; proof, scope, and next action become diffuse.
- **Desired next step:** request a human-reviewed three-point teardown.
- **Offer boundary:** strategy, message, creative direction, implementation
  basis, and QA evidence; no promise of a specific conversion result.
- **Proof available now:** repository method, reusable contracts, a direction
  system, static browser QA evidence, and clearly labelled concept work.
- **Proof not available now:** client outcomes, testimonials, client logos,
  permissioned case studies, production delivery history, or legal clearance.
