# 18 — Trust and proof standards

Proofline sells clarity and a controlled process. It does not manufacture
credibility while the studio is building its own track record.

## Allowed evidence types

1. **Demonstrated method** — the visitor can inspect a teardown, gate, budget,
   receipt, or process artefact.
2. **Stated standard** — the studio names a standard it builds toward and shows
   the evidence, without claiming legal certification.
3. **Permissioned quote** — verbatim feedback with permission, source, and date.
4. **Labelled concept work** — independent demonstration visibly marked as not
   client work.

## Never ship without provenance

Log every logo, quote, metric, screenshot, font, image, voice, likeness, and AI
asset. Unknown rights block shipment. Client material never enters a generative
tool without a reviewed, explicit policy path; the default is not to upload it.

## Case-study rules

- Concept work says it is concept work.
- Redesign studies say they are unaffiliated.
- Client work names the permission date and the exact approved materials.
- Metrics identify the tool, time window, and whether they are client-reported.
- “Not measured” is acceptable.
- No review, logo, award, compliance badge, or result is implied by proximity.

## Ownership rule

The client owns or controls the repository, hosting, domain, analytics, form
destination, content, and exports. Delegated access is documented; credentials
do not belong in chat or the cookbook.
