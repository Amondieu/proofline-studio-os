# Offer catalog

The studio sells bounded outcomes, not an unlimited queue of design requests.
Prices are starting hypotheses for validation, not guarantees or fixed legal
quotes.

## Conversion Starter — €500–750

One focused page, up to seven sections, copy, mobile-first build, inquiry form,
basic metadata, and one revision round in five working days. Best for local
services and early proof. No full brand system, complex integration, or
unlimited revision promise.

## Authority Landing Sprint — €1,250–1,750

The core offer: discovery, message map, visual direction, 8–10 sections,
lead-flow setup, analytics event plan, accessibility/launch QA, two revisions,
and handover in 7–10 working days.

## Launch System — €2,500–3,000

Positioning memo, offer design, two controlled variants, custom visual system,
motion direction, operational routing, tracking plan, performance/accessibility
QA, and 14 days of launch support in 10–14 working days.

## Scope policy

Brand relaunches, e-commerce, membership products, custom app features,
legal drafting, and conversion guarantees are separate scopes. A client can
buy a new scope; it does not silently enter the current sprint.
