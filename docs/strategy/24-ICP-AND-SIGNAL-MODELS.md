# 24 — ICP and signal models

## Priority ICPs

| ICP | Observable gap | Archetype | Likely tier | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| AI / automation consultant | Hype or technical complexity hides the use case and CTA | AI / Automation Authority | Authority Sprint / Launch System | 10 |
| Micro-SaaS / AI tool | Product value is spread across features; demo or trial path is weak | SaaS Launch & Demand | Authority Sprint / Launch System | 9 |
| Premium creative studio | Portfolio is attractive but inquiry fit and process are unclear | Creative Portfolio & Inquiry | Launch System | 8 |
| Boutique B2B consultant | Expertise lacks method, proof, or a clear next step | Expert Authority | Authority Sprint | 8 |
| Premium local service | Mobile trust or quote flow is unclear | High-Trust Lead Engine | Conversion Starter / Authority Sprint | 7 |

“No website” remains a lower-priority hypothesis. It does not establish a
budget, decision-maker, or need for a premium landing system.

## Strong signals

A useful signal combines a current change, a visible conversion gap, and a
plausible decision or budget window.

| Signal | Testable interpretation | Audit route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New service or product announcement | New demand needs a clear explanation | Offer / launch audit |
| Funding, accelerator, or launch communication | Website importance may have increased | SaaS launch audit |
| Active company content with weak website CTA | Attention may not have an owned next step | Content-to-conversion audit |
| New sales, marketing, or product hiring | Go-to-market motion may be changing | Demand-system hypothesis |
| Relaunch or rebrand notice | Message and direction window may be open | Message + direction audit |
| New case study or reference | Proof exists but may not be used in the decision path | Proof architecture audit |
| Hero lacks buyer, outcome, or CTA | Directly observable clarity gap | Three-point teardown |
| Mobile interaction visibly fails | Verifiable technical friction | Mobile conversion audit |
| Offer or pricing page is stale | Positioning may have moved on | Offer clarification audit |

## Weak or invalid signals

- “The website looks old” is taste, not business evidence.
- A missing social account is not a B2B website intent signal.
- A competitor looking better is not a buyer problem.
- A directory listing is a source, not purchase intent.
- “No website” does not prove budget or dissatisfaction.

## Source discipline

Store source URL, source type, collection date, terms review, observation, and
confidence. Prefer the company website, public press/blog/changelog, public
company posts where terms allow, public job pages, permitted directories,
official registers, official APIs, inbound leads, and referrals.

Do not use the source merely because it is easy to automate. A source must be
permitted, relevant, and proportionate to the research question.
