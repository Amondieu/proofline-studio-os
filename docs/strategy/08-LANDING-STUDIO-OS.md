# LANDING-STUDIO-OS

## Positioning

Do not sell “AI-built websites”. Sell a fixed-scope **Conversion Site Sprint**:

> We turn complex offers into clear, high-trust landing pages that make the
> next step obvious.

The studio is paid for offer clarity, conversion copy, visual credibility,
technical implementation, launch QA, and reduced decision load.

## Non-negotiable principles

- One page has one primary conversion.
- One target buyer gets one clear message.
- Copy and message map precede visual production.
- Every CTA is intentional and tested.
- No generic AI gradients, copied competitor language, fake dashboards, or
  template polish without brand logic.
- No launch without mobile, technical, trust, legal, accessibility, and
  consent review.
- No unlimited revisions, guaranteed conversion, or guaranteed compliance.
- Client-owned access and export are part of professional delivery.

## Gate 1 — Discovery

Capture:

- business, market, offer, price/order value, and buying situation;
- specific buyer and expensive current problem;
- alternatives and objections;
- primary conversion and existing tools;
- supplied proof, brand assets, claims constraints, and tone.

If proof, buyer, offer, or conversion is missing, create an **Assumption Map**.
Do not call a concept a case study and do not proceed to final production.

## Gate 2 — Message map

Freeze, in text:

1. target audience;
2. primary job-to-be-done;
3. expensive pain;
4. desired outcome;
5. mechanism;
6. promise without an unsupported guarantee;
7. primary CTA;
8. proof and proof gaps;
9. top objections;
10. required sections.

Client sign-off protects both sides: a later positioning change is a new
decision, not an “easy copy revision”.

## Gate 3 — Direction

Present one screen containing hero, a representative section, typography,
palette, visual/motion principle, and mobile crop. The client chooses one of
the controlled directions. Do not build three full websites for comparison.

## Gate 4 — Build

Build semantically and mobile-first. Test at 360, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440
pixels. Confirm routes, form delivery, metadata, social preview, alt text,
privacy links, consent configuration, asset rights, admin access, and export.

## Gate 5 — QA

Run four review lenses:

- **Mobile conversion:** a new visitor finds audience, offer, and CTA quickly;
- **Technical:** links, forms, routing, calendar, errors, and metadata work;
- **Trust:** proof is supplied, labelled, and not exaggerated;
- **Legal/rights:** privacy, notice, consent, asset permissions, AI disclosure,
  and regulated-claim review are addressed.

## Gate 6 — Launch

The harness may say the project is ready for a human launch review. Only the
named reviewer and client can approve the exact reviewed state. Deployment and
live integrations belong to a separately authorized workflow.
