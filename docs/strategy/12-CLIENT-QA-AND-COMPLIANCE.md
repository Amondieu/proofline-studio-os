# Client QA and compliance

This is a delivery standard, not legal advice. The client remains responsible
for business claims, legal text, consent choices, and rights they supply.

## Accessibility baseline

Target WCAG 2.2 AA practices: readable contrast, keyboard operation, visible
focus, clear labels and errors, target size, reduced-motion support, no content
hidden behind overlays, and meaningful alternative text. Human review is still
required; a checklist is not certification.

## Content and proof

- Testimonials, client logos, numbers, credentials, and case studies require
  traceable source and permission.
- Synthetic visuals cannot imply a real customer, team, product result, or
  event without clear context.
- Health, finance, legal, recruiting, politics, and sensitive-personal-data
  projects receive an additional human specialist/legal review.
- The studio never says “GDPR compliant guaranteed”. It can implement a
  technical baseline and provide a review checklist.

## Ownership and privacy

Confirm who owns domain, hosting, analytics, consent tooling, CRM, source code,
assets, and admin accounts. Do not place private CRM exports, credentials,
health data, financial data, unpublished roadmaps, or unapproved faces/voices
into creative tools.

## Launch test

Do not hand off the launch review until mobile conversion, technical behavior,
trust/proof, legal links, rights, consent, and backup/export are documented.
