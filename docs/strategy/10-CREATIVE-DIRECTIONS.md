# Creative direction system

The directions are a controlled vocabulary for art direction, not three
unrelated brands.

## Precision System

For SaaS, AI, automation, analytics, and complex B2B offers. Calm authority,
structured grids, editorial technology, one signal accent, and restrained
motion. The visual metaphor is complexity becoming a clear decision path.

## Cinematic Authority

For ambitious launches, creative studios, and bold brands. Dark field, ivory
type, sculptural object, controlled contrast, and a short optional motion loop.
The visual metaphor is an offer receiving a stage instead of another page.

## Editorial Luxury

For premium advisory, architecture, hospitality, and high-trust services.
Material texture, quiet typography, generous space, and near-invisible motion.
The visual metaphor is clarity expressed through restraint.

## Asset rules

- Reserve negative space for copy and CTA.
- Never create fake client evidence, product metrics, dashboards, or people.
- Mark synthetic realistic people or scenes where a viewer could mistake them
  for real people or events.
- Record the asset source, rights status, intended use, and approval.
- Keep heavy video optional; the page must communicate without motion.
