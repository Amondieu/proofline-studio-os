# 25 — Prospect scoring

## Score

Every candidate receives a 0–100 score:

| Dimension | Maximum |
| --- | ---: |
| ICP fit | 20 |
| Visible conversion leak | 20 |
| Current trigger | 15 |
| Offer economics | 15 |
| Reachable relevant decision-maker | 10 |
| Evidence confidence | 10 |
| Outreach compliance fit | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Only scores of 70 or higher are eligible for a human audit. The threshold is a
triage rule, not an estimate of conversion probability.

## Interpretation

- **85–100:** strong audit candidate if provenance and compliance are clean.
- **70–84:** manual review required; document the main uncertainty.
- **0–69:** hold or reject; do not spend audit capacity.

An excellent score cannot override an opt-out, unclear source, guessed contact,
regulated claim risk, competitor conflict, or purely subjective design opinion.

## Hard reject or hold

- no relevant professional contact route;
- no clear product/service value;
- source provenance or usage terms unclear;
- recipient role unrelated to the proposed service;
- existing opt-out or suppression flag;
- competitor or reseller conflict without a partnership rationale;
- only “problem” is visual taste;
- audit would require invented proof, metrics, or assumptions;
- high-risk claim area without a reviewable owner.

## Score record rule

The typed model checks that `total` equals the seven component scores. A score
does not authorize research export, email drafting, or sending. Each component
needs a short rationale and at least one relevant evidence trail.

## Controlled learning

Change only one variable per pilot review: ICP, trigger, subject, CTA, or audit
format. Track positive replies, negative replies, opt-outs, bounces, meetings,
qualified calls, and source quality. Do not optimize for volume before the
evidence and suppression loop are trustworthy.
