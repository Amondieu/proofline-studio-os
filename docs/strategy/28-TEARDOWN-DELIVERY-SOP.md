# 28 — Three-point teardown delivery SOP

## Purpose

Deliver a small, useful audit that demonstrates Proofline thinking without
performing a free redesign or making unsupported performance claims.

## Procedure

1. **Confirm candidate** — score at least 70, verify the archetype hypothesis,
   professional relevance, source, and suppression state.
2. **Collect evidence** — record URL, page context, date, observation, and
   confidence. Use no login, private data, or copied restricted material.
3. **Select three leaks** — one clarity, one trust/proof, and one next-step or
   mobile/functionality observation where supported.
4. **Draft one hero decision** — offer, buyer, outcome, and CTA; label it as a
   proposal, not a claim about their business.
5. **Draft one architecture** — show a concise path from message to action.
6. **Run human review** — remove taste-only criticism, unsupported outcomes,
   invented facts, or rights-unclear screenshots.
7. **Package** — use the teardown template or approved Canva deck; include the
   source trail and a clear next step.
8. **Record delivery** — create an `AuditRecord`; do not mark it delivered
   before the human reviewer confirms the exact artifact.

## Quality bar

- maximum three findings;
- every finding has an evidence URL;
- observations and recommendations are separated;
- no guaranteed conversion outcome;
- no fake benchmark or client proof;
- no unlicensed or sensitive screenshot;
- one relevant offer and one low-risk next step;
- recipient may decline without pressure.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the page is inaccessible, evidence is ambiguous, rights are unclear,
the issue is only subjective taste, or the audit would require guessing the
business’s economics or internal data.
