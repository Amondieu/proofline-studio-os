# 22 — Archetype sales playbook

## Public positioning

The master site should make the idea tangible with two visible pilot systems:

1. **AI / Automation Authority** — for complex technical offers that need a
   credible assessment path.
2. **SaaS Launch & Demand** — for focused product launches that need a clear
   use-case and demo path.

The public card CTA is a teardown or fit conversation, not an automatic
purchase. The site should say that the final archetype is confirmed in
Discovery. This avoids selling all five systems before the studio has enough
proof to support that promise.

## Qualification questions

- What is the buyer trying to decide in the first serious interaction?
- What event makes the buyer look for help now?
- What evidence can the client legally and factually show?
- What is the primary conversion and who owns the response?
- Where is the largest trust, privacy, implementation, or switching risk?
- Which constraints would make the recommended tier or direction unsafe?

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response boundary |
| --- | --- |
| “Can you use the same system for any business?” | We reuse the operating method; the archetype is chosen from the buyer job and evidence, not the industry label. |
| “Can you guarantee more leads?” | No. We can define a clearer decision path, evidence plan, and measurable review scope; outcomes remain a hypothesis. |
| “Can we skip Discovery if the template fits?” | No. Discovery confirms the buyer, offer, proof, constraints, and human approvals. |
| “Can you add impressive AI visuals?” | We can propose visuals, but factuality, rights, accessibility, and production readiness remain human-reviewed. |
| “Can you write the legal or compliance claims?” | We can structure the page and flag requirements; the client or counsel supplies and approves legal text. |

## Offer mapping

The catalog recommends an offer tier, but the offer catalog remains the source
of truth for scope and exclusions:

- `conversion-starter`: focused page with a simple, clear offer.
- `authority-sprint`: message, trust architecture, custom direction, and QA for
  a high-consideration offer.
- `launch-system`: positioning, variants, richer assets, routing, performance,
  and post-launch support for a defined launch.

## Internal sales record

Use `templates/archetype-teardown.md` after a first review. Record the observed
leaks and the candidate archetype separately. Never phrase a recommendation as
an observed business fact until the client confirms it.
