# Launch review

## Mobile conversion

- [ ] An external person can identify the offer, audience, and CTA quickly
- [ ] The primary CTA is visible and usable at mobile widths
- [ ] Focus, errors, overlays, and cookie controls do not hide the next action

## Technical

- [ ] Links, forms, delivery, calendar, metadata, redirects, and error states
- [ ] Performance checked on a constrained mobile connection
- [ ] Keyboard path tested from navigation through form completion
- [ ] Reduced-motion behavior checked

## Trust

- [ ] Proof is supplied, labelled, and permissioned
- [ ] No fabricated testimonials, logos, metrics, dashboards, or guarantees
- [ ] Concept work is clearly labelled as concept work

## Legal / rights

- [ ] Privacy notice and legal notice links are present
- [ ] Consent and analytics behavior is reviewed
- [ ] Asset, font, image, likeness, and voice rights are documented
- [ ] Regulated claims received specialist review where applicable
