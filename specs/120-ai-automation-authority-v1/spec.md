# Feature 120 — AI / Automation Authority archetype V1

Status: `proposed / dependent on Features 100 and 110`

Create the first archetype-specific proof-of-method page for technical AI and
automation offers whose value is difficult for first-time visitors to
understand.

## Requirements

- FR-120-01: Hero identifies the buyer and a qualified decision outcome.
- FR-120-02: Page shows buyer pains and workflow/use-case friction.
- FR-120-03: Page explains method and operational-risk treatment.
- FR-120-04: Proof is evidence-backed or explicitly qualified.
- FR-120-05: A visible Studio Concept disclosure is present.
- FR-120-06: Precision System is the proposed default; selection is a human gate.
- FR-120-07: No people, fake dashboards, logos, metrics, testimonials, ROI
  guarantees, or compliance badges are used as proof.
- FR-120-08: Page has a still-first, reduced-motion-safe hero path.
- FR-120-09: CTA remains interest-only and delegates to Feature 130.

See `quality-profile.md`, `pattern-allowlist.md`, and
`anti-pattern-blocklist.md` for the review inputs.
