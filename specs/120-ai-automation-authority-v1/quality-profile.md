# Quality profile — AI / Automation Authority

## Buyer and job

Primary buyer: founder, COO, operations leader, or technical-service owner
evaluating an AI/automation engagement. Job: turn technical capability into
enough clarity and trust to earn a qualified workflow-assessment conversation.

## Required evidence

Supplied scope and assumptions, workflow examples framed as illustrative unless
sourced, Proofline method and QA artifacts, concept labels, and provenance for
every non-code asset.

## Forbidden claims

Unverified ROI, conversion, revenue, legal, accessibility, SEO, compliance,
security, or performance guarantees; fake clients, staff, logos, metrics,
testimonials, awards, dashboards, or generated people as proof.

## Minimum interaction quality

Semantic landmarks and headings, keyboard operation, visible/unobscured focus,
adequate contrast, persistent labels, accessible states, reduced motion,
responsive safe areas, and a still hero fallback.
