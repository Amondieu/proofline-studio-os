# Anti-pattern blocklist — AI / Automation Authority

Automatic blocker if present:

- generic robot or neon cyberpunk imagery;
- fake dashboard, graph, metric, ROI number, or customer result;
- generated person represented as staff, customer, or testimonial;
- logo or brand imitation without rights record;
- compliance/security/legal badge without verified authority;
- CTA implying payment, booking, or guaranteed response when not configured;
- form sending before privacy, retention, routing, and business review;
- motion required to understand content or no reduced-motion/still path;
- concept work presented as commissioned client work.
