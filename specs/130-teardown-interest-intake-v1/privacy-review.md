Status: `blocked / review required`

identity, fields, purpose, lawful basis, processor/provider, hosting, transfer,
access roles, retention, deletion, export, incident path, notification mailbox,
spam/failure behavior, privacy notice, withdrawal, rights language, and the
Austria business/website-information route where applicable.

Until confirmed, keep the interaction non-sending. This document is not legal
advice and is not a privacy notice.
