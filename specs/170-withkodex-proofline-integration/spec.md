# WITHKODEX × Proofline integration

Status: proposed / route specification only

## Objective

Define Proofline Studio as a clearly scoped conversion-systems capability under
the WITHKODEX parent brand without weakening the parent brand's infrastructure,
data-product, or technical-services positioning.

This specification governs the content and handoff boundary. It does not
modify the external WITHKODEX website or claim that its current pages, routes,
ownership, or legal state have been verified.

## Primary user

A B2B founder or operator with a complex AI, SaaS, or technical-service offer
who needs a clear, high-trust conversion surface.

## User outcome

Within 30 seconds on the parent site, a visitor can identify:

1. that WITHKODEX has distinct capability lines;
2. whether Proofline fits a website or landing-page need;
3. that Proofline is a fixed-scope, quality-gated delivery system;
4. how to request a teardown or fit conversation.

## Proposed brand architecture

```text
WITHKODEX  — parent technology studio and trust anchor
├── Infrastructure / KORE — governed AI infrastructure and systems
├── Data Products          — datasets, evaluations, and licensing
├── Technical Services     — architecture, integration, and delivery
└── Proofline Studio       — conversion systems and high-trust web surfaces
```

Proofline is a route and capability line first. A separate domain, independent
social presence, or fully independent brand is deferred until real demand,
references, and delivery evidence justify the added identity and operations.

## Route proposal

Recommended first route: `withkodex.com/proofline`.

| Route | Visitor job | Primary CTA | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Understand WITHKODEX | Explore capabilities / start conversation | Parent brand, no Proofline package wall |
| `/infrastructure` | Assess agent, MCP, memory, or retrieval needs | Discuss infrastructure | No landing-page package as primary offer |
| `/datasets` | Assess data/evaluation product fit | Explore datasets / request access | No web-design messaging |
| `/services` | Assess architecture or custom delivery | Discuss a system | No teardown as primary action |
| `/proofline` | Assess website/conversion-system fit | Request a 3-point teardown | No unrelated infrastructure feature dump |
| `/proofline/teardown` | Request a concrete audit | Submit interest/request | Interest-only until privacy and routing gates clear |

The route name, live navigation, domain ownership, and existing parent-page
content must be confirmed by the WITHKODEX owner before implementation.

## Non-negotiables

- Preserve WITHKODEX as the parent brand.
- Give each capability route one primary user job and one primary CTA.
- Do not present Proofline Studio concepts as client work.
- Do not claim conversion, legal, accessibility, SEO, or revenue guarantees.
- Preserve existing infrastructure, dataset, and technical-services routes.
- Make interactive components keyboard operable and mobile-readable.
- Keep the teardown form disabled or interest-only until privacy and data-routing
  gates are approved.
- No paid checkout, paid project start, invoice, or contract acceptance until
  [`G-BIZ-001`](../../legal/BUSINESS-REGISTRATION-GATE.v1.md) is cleared.
- Do not invent WITHKODEX products, capabilities, case studies, metrics,
  ownership, or legal identity; verify each claim with the parent owner.

## Scope

In scope:

- parent/child brand architecture;
- route and navigation contract;
- Proofline capability-page message boundary;
- CTA and interest-form boundary;
- cross-link and ownership requirements;
- acceptance, traceability, and QA records.

Out of scope:

- rebuilding or deploying the external WITHKODEX site;
- choosing a final domain or hosting provider;
- writing final legal or privacy pages;
- activating analytics, CRM, calendar, or payment integrations;
- asserting KORE, dataset, or technical-service product facts without a verified
  source record;
- splitting Proofline into a separate brand or legal entity.

## Proofline route content contract

Suggested page promise:

> Conversion systems for complex offers.

Supporting message:

> Proofline turns hard-to-explain B2B services and software into clear,
> high-trust landing pages — message, design, build, and launch-quality checks
> in one fixed-scope sprint.

Required sections:

1. audience/problem and decision outcome;
2. the two first pilot archetypes: AI / Automation Authority and SaaS Launch &
   Demand;
3. Message → Direction → Build → QA → Launch/Handover;
4. quality system: mobile, keyboard, form routing, rights/proof integrity, and
   ownership;
5. clearly labelled studio concepts;
6. founding-client interest or teardown CTA with status disclosure.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001:** Each parent capability route has a distinct visitor job, owner,
  source record, and primary CTA.
- **AC-002:** `/proofline` communicates audience, offer, outcome, and primary
  CTA without requiring a technical WITHKODEX explanation first.
- **AC-003:** No route has more than one primary conversion action.
- **AC-004:** Concept and redesign work are labelled adjacent to the relevant
  portfolio item.
- **AC-005:** Navigation and mobile navigation have a keyboard-operable path.
- **AC-006:** The teardown form is either disabled or explicitly interest-only
  until privacy, retention, routing, and business gates are approved.
- **AC-007:** Parent-brand claims and route ownership are verified by a human;
  no external-site fact is inferred from this spec.
- **AC-008:** The Proofline route preserves the existing human-gated pipeline
  and does not authorize paid work or launch.
- **AC-009:** Austria business/legal gates remain linked and blocking.
