# Feature 100 — WITHKODEX portfolio master site V1

Status: `proposed / planning-only`

## Objective

Define the smallest parent-brand vertical slice that lets a visitor understand
WITHKODEX, find Proofline, and continue into the first archetype without
pretending that external parent-site facts or production operations are
verified.

## Scope

Routes:

- `/` — parent-brand hero, four capability routes, principles, and interest CTA;
- `/proofline` — Proofline offer, quality system, and archetype entry;
- `/proofline/ai-automation-authority` — first archetype;
- `/proofline/teardown` — interest-only request form;
- `/legal` and `/privacy` — reviewed placeholders, not invented legal text.

The external WITHKODEX site is not modified or treated as verified by this
feature.

## Requirements

- FR-100-01: Each route has one visitor job and one primary CTA.
- FR-100-02: Proofline is presented as a capability line under WITHKODEX.
- FR-100-03: Parent-brand claims are marked unverified until the owner confirms
  them through a source record.
- FR-100-04: Archetype work is labelled studio concept/proof of method, not
  client outcome proof.
- FR-100-05: Teardown is interest-only, minimal-data, non-payment, and
  non-booking.
- FR-100-06: No analytics, CRM, login, CMS, chatbot, or provider is enabled by
  this feature.
- FR-100-07: Semantic HTML, keyboard operation, mobile layout, focus safety,
  contrast, and reduced motion are acceptance requirements.
- FR-100-08: Higgsfield assets remain optional, provenance-recorded, and
  human-gated.
- FR-100-09: The Studio pipeline and launch blockers remain authoritative.

## Out of scope

Paid checkout, client login/portal, autonomous outreach, broad CRM sharing,
additional live archetypes, full CMS, tracking before consent design, chatbot,
heavy WebGL, and public deployment with unfinished legal routes.
